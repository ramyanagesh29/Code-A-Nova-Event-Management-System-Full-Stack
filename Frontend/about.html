About Our Event Management System

The Event Management System is a full-stack web application designed to simplify the process of organising and participating in events. Students can explore upcoming events, register online, and manage their registrations through their dashboard.

Our Purpose

Our goal is to provide a simple and organised platform where students can discover technical events, workshops, seminars, hackathons, and other learning opportunities.

What You Can Do

Students can create an account, log in securely, browse events, register for available events, receive confirmation emails, view their registrations, and cancel registrations when needed.

Administrators can create, update and delete events, monitor registrations, view students, and track event statistics from the admin dashboard.
